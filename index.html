Two easy ways to tuck him into Codex.

Do it yourself

  1. Download and unzip the Teddy ZIP.
  2. Open the unzipped `teddy` folder.
  3. Copy `pet.json` and `spritesheet.webp` into `~/.codex/pets/teddy/`.
  4. Restart Codex if Teddy does not appear.
  5. Select `Teddy V3.1` in Codex pets.
